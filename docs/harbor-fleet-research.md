## 2. Sourced fleet lists by operator

In the tables below, **AIS name if different** gives the 20-character uppercase AIS broadcast string whenever it differs from the operator's prose vessel name rotated to uppercase (for example, due to the 20-character AIS field limit, punctuation differences, or abbreviated titles). Where the 20-character uppercase string is identical to the operator's name in uppercase, `Same` is shown alongside the uppercase string.

### 2.1 NY Waterway

NY Waterway (`Port Imperial Ferry Corp.`, FCC FRN `0007000656`, and affiliate `Billybey Ferry Company, LLC`, FCC FRN `0013250691`, headquartered at 4800 Avenue at Port Imperial, Weehawken, NJ) operates commuter ferry routes between New Jersey (Weehawken Port Imperial, Hoboken NJ Transit Terminal, Hoboken 14th Street, Lincoln Harbor, Edgewater, Paulus Hook, Liberty Harbor, Port Liberté, South Amboy, and the **Belford / Middletown** route) and Manhattan (Midtown West 39th Street, Brookfield Place / Battery Park City, and Pier 11 / Wall Street), as well as the **IKEA Red Hook** shuttle from Pier 11 and contract operations for the Trust for Governors Island [1][19][20][23]. It also operates the two Paulus Hook–Brookfield Place catamarans (*Jersey* and *York*) owned by Hudson Ferry Corp. / Goldman Sachs [1][2][6][23].

Because `www.nywaterway.com` refused automated HTTP requests (`HTTP 403`), the fleet below is compiled from active and historical FCC ULS ship station licences under FRN `0007000656` and FRN `0013250691` [1], USCG PSIX vessel documentation and inspection records [2], shipyard construction records from Gulf Craft, Gladding-Hearn, Allen Marine, All American Marine, Yank Marine, and Blount Boats [3][4][5][6][7], *WorkBoat* reports [19][20], and the Wikipedia fleet list [23].

#### Record discrepancies for NY Waterway
1. ***Port Imperial New Jersey* (`928331`) vs *Governor Thomas H. Kean* (`1137612`)**: In FCC ULS [1], Port Imperial Ferry Corp. holds an active licence `WDK7329` (granted 2019-03-26, MMSI `368083360`) with vessel name `Port Imperial New Jersey`, but the `docnum` field on that licence is `1137612`—which USCG PSIX [2] and Allen Marine's build table [5] prove is the official number of the 2003 Allen Marine 78 ft catamaran *Governor Thomas H. Kean* (which also has its own active FCC licence `WDH8185`, MMSI `366902350`). Meanwhile, USCG PSIX shows `928331` (*Port Imperial New Jersey*, 1988 Gulf Craft Hull 328) has no valid COI or COD. We believe `928331` is the true official number of *Port Imperial New Jersey* (now retired) and `1137612` is a clerical error on FCC licence `WDK7329`.
2. **Duplicate active FCC licences for *Robert Fulton* (`990941`) and *Jackie Robinson* (`1347727`)**:
   - *Robert Fulton* (`990941`) has two active FCC licences under FRN `0007000656`: `WBS7886` (MMSI `366702000`, granted 2018-04-03, `docnum` blank) and `WDK7530` (MMSI `368085280`, granted 2019-04-03, `docnum` `990941`) [1]. USCG PSIX lists `WBS7886` as the vessel's call sign [2]. Both MMSIs should be associated with the vessel until confirmed on AIS.
   - *Jackie Robinson* (`1347727`) has two active FCC licences granted two weeks apart in late 2024: `WDP8468` (MMSI `368390760`, granted 2024-10-16) and `WDP8675` (MMSI `368392620`, granted 2024-11-01), both listing `docnum` `1347727` [1].
3. **Dual active FCC licences for *Christopher Columbus* (`1100520`)**: Port Imperial Ferry Corp. holds active licence `WDH6958` (MMSI `366863240`, renewed 2024-10-08), while `HNY Ferry LLC` (NYC Ferry) still holds an active licence `WDK4776` (MMSI `368058590`, granted 2018-10-02 during NYC Ferry's early charter period) [1]. #### Record discrepancies for NYC Ferry
1. **Class and dimensions of `H-114` (*Jewel of the Harbor*, `1293319`), `H-115` (*Dream Boat*, `1293320`), `H-214` (*Koalafied Cruiser*, `1293327`), and `H-215` (*Signs to Liberty*, `1296862`)**:
   - Metal Shark's press release [35] and NYCEDC's FY24 report [18] state that Metal Shark built sixteen 150-passenger 85 ft Subchapter T vessels and six 350-passenger 97 ft Subchapter K vessels (`HB 108`..`HB 113` or `HB 90`), and that the total 38-vessel fleet comprises twenty-six 150-passenger vessels and twelve 350-passenger vessels.
   - However, in USCG PSIX [2], `1293319`, `1293320`, `1293327`, and `1296862` all have registered length `90.70 ft x 27.90 ft x 11.50 ft` and 93 GT Regulatory / 228 GT Convention—the exact hull dimensions of the 97 ft LOA hull (`H-090`, `H-091`, `H-092`), rather than the `80.40 ft x 26.20 ft x 9.20 ft` (90 GT) dimensions of the 2017–2018 85 ft hulls. In `shipbuildinghistory.com` (`small_metalshark.htm` and `small_horizon.htm`) [30][31], `HB 114` is listed as 95 ft / 93 GT while `HB 115`, `H 214`, and `H 215` are listed as 85 ft / 90 GT. Wikipedia [37] lists `H-114`, `H-115`, `H-214`, and `H-215` as 150-passenger T-class vessels (matching NYCEDC's count of twenty-six 150-passenger and twelve 350-passenger vessels). We report them as 150-passenger T-class vessels per NYCEDC and Wikipedia while noting their 90.7 ft registered hull length in USCG PSIX.

Live harbor AIS observation should verify whether any additional vessels use abbreviations (such as `SEN` or `CAPT` or omitted spaces like `GOVERNORS1`).
